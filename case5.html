Case Study 5: Hospital Expansion

Case 5 of 8

Context

A hospital chain plans to expand into Tier-2/3 cities. Current occupancy is 65% in metros. Challenges include talent availability, infrastructure cost, and maintaining quality standards.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize expansion opportunity, constraints, and strategic recommendations.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare hospital chains on pricing, services, and operational efficiency.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze patient journey from admission to discharge and identify bottlenecks.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Evaluate demand in Tier-2/3 cities and macro healthcare trends.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Develop strategy to scale while maintaining quality and profitability.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Define expansion roadmap including infrastructure and staffing.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify risks such as talent shortage and quality control issues.
  • 8. Operational Improvements: Suggest efficiency improvements in hospital operations.
