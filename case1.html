Case Study 1

Digital Insurance Growth Strategy

Digital Insurance Growth

Case 1 of 8

Context

A mid-sized Indian private bank with ~5M customers and 3M MAUs is struggling to scale insurance sales. Current penetration is 8% vs industry benchmark of ~20%.

Insurance products are available but journeys are fragmented, personalization is weak, and cross-sell is underutilized. Leadership aims to double penetration in 2 years.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize the problem statement, key insights, recommended strategy, and expected business impact in a concise manner.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare the bank with leading private banks and insurtech players across product offerings, digital journeys, personalization, and conversion metrics.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze the insurance distribution value chain (product → onboarding → servicing → claims → renewal) and identify key inefficiencies and improvement areas.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Estimate the addressable market opportunity and analyze macro-environmental factors along with internal strengths and weaknesses.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Prepare a structured slide outlining key initiatives, solution design, and expected impact on customer engagement and revenue.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Develop a phased roadmap with timelines, key milestones, and ownership across teams.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify key risks (regulatory, operational, adoption) and propose mitigation strategies.
  • 8. KPIs & Success Metrics: Define measurable KPIs such as conversion rates, attach rate, retention, and customer lifetime value.
