Case Study 1

Digital Insurance Growth Strategy

Digital Insurance Growth

Case 1 of 8

Business Transformation Case Study

Context

A mid-sized Indian private bank with ~5M customers and 3M monthly active users (MAUs) is struggling to scale insurance sales through its digital channels. Current insurance penetration is ~8%, significantly lower than the industry benchmark of ~20% achieved by leading private banks and insurtech platforms.

The bank currently offers life, health, and motor insurance products through multiple insurer partnerships. However, the customer journey is highly fragmented, personalization capabilities are limited, and cross-sell opportunities across savings accounts, loans, and credit cards remain underutilized.

Leadership has identified insurance as a major strategic growth lever and aims to double penetration over the next two years while improving customer engagement, digital adoption, and revenue contribution from fee-based products.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize the problem statement, strategic opportunities, recommended initiatives, and expected business impact.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare the bank with leading private banks and insurtech players across product offerings, digital journeys, personalization capabilities, and conversion metrics.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze the insurance distribution value chain including product design, onboarding, servicing, claims management, and renewal processes to identify inefficiencies.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Estimate the addressable insurance market opportunity and evaluate macroeconomic, regulatory, technological, and competitive factors impacting growth.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Develop a structured strategy outlining digital initiatives, customer engagement levers, personalization capabilities, and operational improvements.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Create a phased execution roadmap including timelines, ownership, technology enablement, and key milestones.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify risks related to regulation, customer adoption, operational scalability, and insurer dependency along with mitigation strategies.
  • 8. KPIs & Success Metrics: Define measurable KPIs such as conversion rate, attach rate, retention, customer lifetime value, and revenue contribution.
