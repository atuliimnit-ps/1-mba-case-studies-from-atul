Case Study 8: FMCG Rural Penetration

Case 8 of 8

Context

An FMCG company wants to grow rural market share. Rural contributes 40% revenue but growth is slowing. Distribution gaps and affordability issues exist.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize growth challenges and recommended rural strategy.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare with competitors on distribution and product strategy.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze manufacturing, distribution, and retail execution.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Evaluate rural demand trends and macroeconomic factors.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Design strategy to improve penetration and affordability.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Outline rollout strategy across regions and channels.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify risks such as supply chain and pricing challenges.
  • 8. Distribution Strategy: Recommend improvements in last-mile reach and channel efficiency.
