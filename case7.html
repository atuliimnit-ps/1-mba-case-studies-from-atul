Case Study 7: Airline Profitability

Case 7 of 8

Context

An airline is facing margin pressure due to rising fuel costs (40% of total cost). Load factor is 82%, but profitability is declining.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize profitability challenges and recommended strategic actions.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare cost structures, pricing, and operational efficiency.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze operations from ticketing to flight operations and turnaround.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Assess industry trends, fuel pricing impact, and competition.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Propose initiatives to improve profitability and efficiency.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Develop roadmap with short-term and long-term actions.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify risks such as fuel volatility and demand fluctuations.
  • 8. Cost Reduction Strategy: Recommend actionable cost optimization measures.
