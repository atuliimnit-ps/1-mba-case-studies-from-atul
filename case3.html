EV Adoption & Growth Strategy

EV Adoption Strategy

Case 3 of 8

Business Transformation Case Study

Context

An Indian automobile OEM aims to significantly increase EV adoption over the next five years. Currently, electric vehicles contribute only ~6% of the company’s total annual sales despite strong government focus on sustainability and clean mobility.

The company has launched multiple EV models across hatchback and SUV categories, but customer adoption remains lower than expected. Key barriers include inadequate charging infrastructure, higher upfront pricing (~30% premium compared to ICE vehicles), battery range anxiety, and limited consumer awareness.

The leadership team believes the EV market in India will grow rapidly due to government incentives, increasing fuel prices, and rising environmental awareness. However, competition from both domestic and global players is intensifying, and profitability remains a concern due to high battery costs and supply chain dependency.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize the current adoption challenges, growth opportunities, strategic recommendations, and expected business impact.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare the company’s EV portfolio with leading automotive players across pricing, charging ecosystem, product range, technology, and customer adoption strategies.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze the EV value chain including battery sourcing, manufacturing, assembly, distribution, charging ecosystem, and after-sales service.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Estimate the EV market opportunity and evaluate macroeconomic, technological, regulatory, and competitive factors influencing EV adoption.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Develop a strategy to improve EV adoption through pricing innovation, infrastructure partnerships, financing models, and customer awareness initiatives.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Create a phased roadmap outlining product launches, charging infrastructure expansion, partnerships, and operational milestones.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify risks related to supply chain dependency, battery costs, infrastructure readiness, and customer adoption, along with mitigation strategies.
  • 8. Pricing Strategy: Recommend pricing, financing, and subscription-based models to improve affordability and customer acquisition.
