Case Study 3: EV Adoption Strategy

Case 3 of 8

Context

An Indian automobile OEM aims to increase EV adoption. EVs currently contribute only 6% of total sales. Key barriers include charging infrastructure, pricing (~30% premium vs ICE), and consumer awareness.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize adoption barriers, opportunities, and strategic recommendations.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare EV offerings, pricing, charging ecosystem, and customer adoption strategies.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze battery sourcing, manufacturing, distribution, and after-sales service.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Estimate EV market growth potential and analyze regulatory and technological drivers.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Develop initiatives to drive adoption across pricing, infrastructure, and awareness.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Create a phased roadmap for scaling EV sales and ecosystem development.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify risks related to infrastructure, cost, and customer adoption.
  • 8. Pricing Strategy: Recommend pricing and financing strategies to improve affordability.
