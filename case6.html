SaaS Global Expansion Strategy

SaaS Global Expansion Strategy

Case 6 of 8

Business Transformation Case Study

Context

An Indian B2B SaaS company with Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) of ~$10M is planning to expand into global markets including the United States and Europe over the next three years. The company provides workflow automation and analytics solutions to enterprise clients across BFSI, retail, and healthcare sectors.

While the company has achieved strong growth in the domestic market due to competitive pricing and product flexibility, global expansion presents new challenges. These include strong competition from established global SaaS players, customer acquisition costs in developed markets, localization requirements, data privacy regulations, and building enterprise sales capabilities.

Leadership believes international markets can significantly increase revenue and valuation multiples. However, the organization must carefully prioritize markets, optimize pricing strategy, strengthen partnerships, and scale operations without impacting profitability.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize the global expansion opportunity, key business challenges, strategic recommendations, and expected business impact.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare the company with leading global SaaS players across product capabilities, pricing models, go-to-market strategies, customer support, and scalability.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze the end-to-end SaaS value chain including product development, cloud infrastructure, sales, onboarding, customer success, and support operations.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Estimate the addressable global SaaS market opportunity and evaluate macroeconomic, regulatory, technological, and competitive factors affecting expansion.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Develop a structured strategy for market entry, localization, partnership development, customer acquisition, and scalable growth.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Create a phased roadmap covering market prioritization, team expansion, product localization, partnership setup, and operational milestones.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify risks related to compliance, cybersecurity, competition, customer acquisition costs, and operational scalability along with mitigation measures.
  • 8. Pricing Strategy: Recommend pricing, packaging, subscription, and enterprise contract strategies for global markets.
