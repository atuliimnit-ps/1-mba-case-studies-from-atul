Case Study 6: SaaS Global Expansion

Case 6 of 8

Context

An Indian SaaS company with $10M ARR wants to expand to US and Europe. Challenges include localization, pricing strategy, and strong competition.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize expansion strategy, challenges, and expected outcomes.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare with global SaaS players on features, pricing, and GTM strategies.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze product development, sales funnel, and customer support processes.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Evaluate global SaaS opportunity and regulatory environment.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Recommend strategy for market entry and growth.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Define rollout strategy including localization and partnerships.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify risks related to competition, compliance, and scaling.
  • 8. Pricing Strategy: Recommend pricing and packaging strategy for global markets.
