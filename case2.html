Quick Commerce Expansion Strategy

Quick Commerce Expansion

Case 2 of 8

Business Transformation Case Study

Context

A quick commerce startup operating in Tier-1 cities wants to expand into Tier-2 markets. Current delivery promise is 10–15 minutes, but unit economics are negative due to high last-mile costs and low AOV (~₹450).

The company currently operates 120 dark stores across major metropolitan cities and has experienced rapid order growth over the last two years. However, increasing competition, rising customer acquisition costs, and pressure from investors to improve profitability have created strategic concerns.

Leadership believes Tier-2 markets offer significant growth potential due to increasing smartphone penetration, rising disposable income, and changing consumer preferences. At the same time, the company fears lower order density, operational inefficiencies, and limited supply chain infrastructure in smaller cities.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize the expansion opportunity, key business challenges, strategic recommendations, and expected business impact.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare the company with leading quick commerce and e-commerce players across delivery speed, assortment strategy, pricing, customer experience, and unit economics.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze sourcing, inventory management, warehousing (dark stores), last-mile delivery, and customer experience to identify inefficiencies and improvement opportunities.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Estimate the Tier-2 market opportunity and evaluate macroeconomic, technological, regulatory, and competitive factors affecting expansion.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Prepare a structured strategy outlining growth initiatives, operational improvements, and profitability levers for sustainable expansion.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Develop a phased rollout roadmap across cities including timelines, resource allocation, and operational milestones.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify risks related to logistics, customer adoption, supply chain disruption, and cost escalation, along with mitigation strategies.
  • 8. Unit Economics Analysis: Evaluate cost structure, contribution margins, breakeven timeline, and recommendations to improve profitability.
