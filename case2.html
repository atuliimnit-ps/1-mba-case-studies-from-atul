Case Study 2: Quick Commerce Expansion

Case 2 of 8

Context

A quick commerce startup operating in Tier-1 cities wants to expand into Tier-2 markets. Current delivery promise is 10–15 minutes, but unit economics are negative due to high last-mile costs and low AOV (~₹450).

Leadership believes Tier-2 markets offer scale but fears lower demand density and higher logistics inefficiencies.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize expansion opportunity, challenges, and recommended strategic direction.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare with leading players on delivery speed, pricing, assortment, and unit economics.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze sourcing, warehousing (dark stores), last-mile delivery, and customer experience to identify inefficiencies.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Estimate Tier-2 market opportunity and assess macro trends and competitive positioning.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Design a strategy balancing growth, profitability, and operational efficiency.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Define rollout strategy across cities with clear milestones and scalability considerations.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify risks such as demand variability, cost overruns, and logistics constraints.
  • 8. Unit Economics Analysis: Evaluate cost structure, contribution margins, and breakeven timeline.
