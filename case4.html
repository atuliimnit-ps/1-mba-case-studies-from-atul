Case Study 4: EdTech Turnaround

Case 4 of 8

Context

An EdTech firm is facing declining revenues and rising CAC. Conversion rates have dropped by 30%. High marketing spends and low retention are key concerns.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize business challenges, key insights, and turnaround strategy.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare product offerings, pricing models, and customer acquisition strategies.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Evaluate content creation, marketing, sales, and delivery efficiency.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Assess market trends, regulatory environment, and internal capabilities.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Propose a strategy to improve profitability and customer retention.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Outline execution roadmap with clear timelines and resource allocation.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify risks such as churn, CAC inflation, and regulatory issues.
  • 8. Cost Optimization: Recommend cost reduction levers across marketing and operations.
