Case Study 4

EdTech Profitability Turnaround Strategy

EdTech Turnaround Strategy

Case 4 of 8

Context

An EdTech company operating across K-12 and competitive exam preparation segments is facing declining revenues and increasing profitability pressure. Over the last 18 months, customer acquisition costs (CAC) have increased significantly while conversion rates have dropped by nearly 30%.

The company heavily relies on digital marketing and inside sales teams for customer acquisition. Despite strong brand awareness, retention rates are declining due to increased competition, content commoditization, and changing customer preferences toward affordable and outcome-driven learning models.

Leadership is under pressure from investors to improve profitability, reduce operational inefficiencies, and build a sustainable growth model. At the same time, the organization must maintain growth momentum while responding to regulatory scrutiny and changing market dynamics.

Questions

  • 1. Executive Summary: Summarize the company’s key business challenges, major insights, turnaround opportunities, and expected business impact.
  • 2. Competitive Benchmarking: Compare the company with leading EdTech players across product offerings, pricing models, customer acquisition strategies, retention initiatives, and learning outcomes.
  • 3. Value Chain Analysis: Analyze the end-to-end value chain including content creation, technology platform, marketing, sales, customer onboarding, and learning delivery.
  • 4. Market Size + PESTEL + SWOT: Assess market opportunity, industry trends, regulatory landscape, technological shifts, and the company’s internal strengths and weaknesses.
  • 5. Solution & Approach: Develop a structured turnaround strategy focused on profitability improvement, customer retention, product differentiation, and operational efficiency.
  • 6. Implementation Plan: Create a phased execution roadmap including timelines, ownership, investment priorities, and transformation milestones.
  • 7. Risk Mitigation Plan: Identify key risks such as churn, rising CAC, regulatory changes, competitive disruption, and declining customer trust along with mitigation measures.
  • 8. Cost Optimization: Recommend actionable cost optimization levers across marketing, sales operations, content production, and technology infrastructure.
